# CAP Theorem

CAP theorem says a distributed data store can only guarantee two out of three:
Consistency, Availability, and Partition tolerance. Since network partitions
are basically unavoidable in real systems, in practice you're choosing between
consistency and availability when a partition happens.

See [[Distributed Systems MOC]] and [[Consistency Models]] for more.
